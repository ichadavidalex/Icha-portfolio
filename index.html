Who I Am & Why I Do This

The Mind Behind the Content

I'm Icha David, a Social Media Manager, Brand Strategist, and AI Content Creator who genuinely believes your online presence should feel like you.

I help brands turn their knowledge and ideas into content systems that keep them visible, consistent, and connected to the people who need what they offer.

That means content strategy, social media management, short-form videos, social media design, AI-powered content creation, personal brand positioning, ads, and email automation. I don't just want to help you post more. I want to help you build a system that makes showing up easier and your message easier to remember.

I've worked with brands and personal brands across different industries, including Komot, a travel and visa startup, ATL Sprinters, a rental service, and faith-based Karah Music House. Different audiences, different goals, but the approach stays the same: understand the people you want to reach, then create content that actually speaks to them.

When I'm not creating content or building strategies, you will find me writing and reading fiction on Webnovel, assisting the First Rooted podcast, reading a personal development book, or watching an HBO series, imagining my life as a dragon rider.

Helping meaningful brands show up and grow, wherever they are, based in Nigeria.
